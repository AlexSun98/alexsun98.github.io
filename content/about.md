## How I work

Boring and dependable beats clever. Simplicity is always the deliverable.

I also believe self-evolving agents, the ones that improve their own tools and checks, will end up doing real work in real businesses, not as a demo. They are the things that let a small team deliver far more than its size suggests. I think that’s one of the things that pushed me to build Mirana as the very first version. The story is honestly just beginning.
