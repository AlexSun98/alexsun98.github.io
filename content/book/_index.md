I am writing this in the open, one chapter at a time. Chapters go up when they
are readable, not when they are finished, and the front matter on each one says
which it is.

It is for architects, principal engineers and engineering leaders who are
already past the chat window. Three parts: principles, then architecture and
capabilities, then implementation with the scars left in.
