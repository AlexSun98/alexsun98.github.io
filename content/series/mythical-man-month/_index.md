Brooks wrote *The Mythical Man-Month* in 1975, about what happens when you put people on a late project. Agents were supposed to make it irrelevant. Working with them daily since February 2026, I think the opposite happened.

This goes through the book a chapter at a time. Each one takes a Brooks idea, says what it meant in 1975, and asks what it costs now that the thing on the other end of the task is a model rather than a person.

Chapters go up as they are written, and the order follows Brooks rather than the order I wrote them in.
